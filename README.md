# Business Analytics Projects 📊

A curated collection of real-world Business Analytics & Data Intelligence projects featuring end-to-end exploratory data analysis, business metric modeling, interactive dashboards, and executive reporting.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Organizations across e-commerce, streaming entertainment, and financial services generate vast streams of operational data. Converting raw transactions, video catalog metrics, and revenue figures into actionable business decisions requires robust analytical modeling and interactive dashboard reporting.

## 💡 Solution Architecture
Multi-domain analytics portfolio covering key business intelligence projects:
- **Amazon Sales Data Analysis:** Evaluated sales volume, discount structures, customer ratings, and product category performance using Python & SQL.
- **Prime Video Content Analytics:** Built an interactive Power BI dashboard tracking release trends, content ratings, genres, and geographic distribution.
- **Linear vs. Exponential Growth Modeling:** Constructed Excel analytical models comparing customer acquisition and revenue forecasting methods.
- **Profit Calculation Engine:** Formulated Excel financial models evaluating cost structures, gross profit margins, and net profit performance.

## 🚀 Business & Technical Impact
- **Data Analyst:** Created dynamic Power BI & Tableau visual dashboards translating raw dataset trends into executive-level key metrics.
- **Data Scientist:** Conducted correlation analysis, growth trajectory modeling, and segment-level statistical comparisons.
- **Data Engineer:** Cleaned, transformed, and structured multi-source raw files (CSV, Excel, PBIX) into normalized analytics datasets.
